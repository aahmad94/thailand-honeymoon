Thailand · 11 days

Krabi cliffs first, Koh Lanta beaches after. Andaman dry season — January.

5 nightsKrabi / Railay / Tubkaek
5 nightsKoh Lanta

Andaman limestone water and a long west-coast beach — the two moods of this trip.

10 nights = 11 calendar days. Fly into and out of Krabi (KBV) unless a Phuket fare is clearly better.

Recommended split

Why this order: Land, do the photogenic boat days while energy is high, then slow down on Lanta’s longer beaches.

Flights

In / out Krabi (KBV)
Backup Phuket (HKT)
Pakistan Lahore (LHE) or Islamabad (ISB)

Typical path: LHE → BKK then BKK → KBV (~1h15). Land KBV before mid-afternoon. Leave Lanta 4+ hours before a domestic hop.

Day-by-day

  1. Day 1 — Arrive KBV. Sleep.
  2. Day 2 — Railay / Phra Nang.
  3. Day 3 — Hong Islands or 4 Islands. Halal packed lunch.
  4. Day 4 — Spa. Optional Tiger Cave.
  5. Day 5 — Buffer day.
  6. Day 6 — Transfer to Koh Lanta.
  7. Day 7–8 — Beach and sunset.
  8. Day 9 — Koh Rok if seas are good.
  9. Day 10 — Rest.
  10. Day 11 — Fly home via KBV.

Krabi stays

Pick one

1. Rayavadee — Railay

Boat-only, three beaches. Hotel runs the transfer.

2. Phulay Bay, Ritz-Carlton Reserve — Tubkaek

Road-access villas. 15–20 min to Ao Nang pier.

3. Banyan Tree Krabi — Tubkaek

Pool suites, strong spa, quieter than Ao Nang.

Koh Lanta stays

1. Pimalai — Kantiang Bay

South island flagship. Use hotel transfer from Saladan.

2. Layana — Long Beach (adults-only)

Closer to the pier and dinners off-property.

3. Twin Lotus

Softer price if the first two are sold out.
