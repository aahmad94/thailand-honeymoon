11 nights = 12 calendar days. Target $200–300/night. After Jan 7 is cheaper than Jan 1–6.

Why this split

Ao Nang puts cliff views, the Railay pier, and restaurants in walking distance. Lanta is for longer beaches. Dropped Phulay Bay, Banyan Tree, and Rayavadee — those usually sit well above $300 in January.

Flights and transfers

In / out Krabi (KBV)

LHE or ISB → BKK → KBV. Hotel car to Ao Nang 25–35 min. Longtail to Railay 10–15 min. Van + ferry to Lanta 2.5–3.5 hrs. No rental car.

Day-by-day

  1. Day 1 — Land KBV. Ao Nang sunset and easy dinner.
  2. Day 2 — Railay West + Phra Nang by longtail. Night market after.
  3. Day 3 — Hong Islands. Packed halal / seafood lunch.
  4. Day 4 — Spa morning. Nopparat Thara walk or Tiger Cave.
  5. Day 5 — Buffer day. Mosque-area halal stalls after Maghrib.
  6. Day 6 — Transfer to Koh Lanta. Beach only.
  7. Day 7 — Long Beach sunset dinner in walking distance.
  8. Day 8 — Half-day Lanta Old Town by hotel car.
  9. Day 9 — Koh Rok if seas are calm.
  10. Day 10 — Rest / spa.
  11. Day 11 — Extra beach day.
  12. Day 12 — Saladan → KBV → home.

Krabi — pick one, 5 nights

Target $180–300 Book sea-view or cliff-view, not garden.

1. Avani Ao Nang Cliff — first pick

Cliff / rooftop views toward the Railay karsts. About 500 m to the beach and longtail pier. Night market, cafes, and mosque-area halal food are a 5–12 minute walk. Mid-January sea-view often fits the cap; 1–6 Jan may not.

2. Holiday Inn Resort Krabi Ao Nang Beach

On the sand. Same restaurant strip, easier swim, less wow from the room.

3. Hotel Adam or Pimann Buri pool villas

Plunge pool + short Grab to the beach. Confirm January — some villa dates creep over $300.

Skipped: Phulay Bay, Banyan Tree, Rayavadee. Tubkaak is pretty but isolated and often over budget in January.

Koh Lanta — pick one, 6 nights

Stay north so dinners are walkable.

1. Layana Resort & Spa — Long Beach (adults-only)

Best honeymoon fit in budget. Beachfront sunsets. Often high $100s to ~$300. Walk / short tuk-tuk to other Long Beach restaurants.

2. Avani+ Koh Lanta

Often $120–220. Same north strip, easier if Layana jumps in January.

3. Twin Lotus or Rawi Warin

Quieter. Twin Lotus is adults-only in high season. Rawi Warin has a hill-pool view; use the buggy. Pimalai beach villas usually break $300 in January.
