Krabi split (locked intent)

Order matters Boat days need the pier. Spa days need the suite. Do not reverse this.

Nights 1–3 — Avani Ao Nang Cliff

Walk ~8–15 min to Ao Nang Beach and the longtail pier. Use a mid cliff / sea-view room, not the $2,500 suite. Hotel car from KBV 25–35 min. Town, night market, and strip are on foot.

Nights 4–5 — Anana Ecological Resort, Deluxe Suite

Move after Hong Islands (late Day 3 or morning Day 4). 10–15 min by hotel car / Grab. Better room and spa; shuttle if you go back to town. Ask Anana to hold a late checkout on Day 6 if the Lanta transfer is after noon.

Moving cost: one pack-up, one 15-minute transfer. Worth it only because the two hotels do different jobs.

Day-by-day

  1. Day 1 — Avani — Land KBV (do not use a 85-min BKK connection). Hotel car to Avani. Sunset + walk to dinner.
    Tonight: tell Avani desk you want Hong Islands on Day 3. If you have not already held a boat, book it now.
  2. Day 2 — Avani · Railay — Walk to the pier by 8:00–8:30. Shared longtail 10–15 min to Railay West / Phra Nang. Back before dusk. Night market after.
    Arrange: nothing in advance. Cash at the pier. Last shared boat ~18:00.
  3. Day 3 — Avani · Hong Islands — Pickup ~8:00–8:30 from Avani. Full day, 6–7 hours. Ask for packed lunch (seafood / no pork) when you book.
    Already booked 2–3 days earlier. Do not walk up on the morning of Jan 12/13.
  4. Day 4 — move to Anana · spa — Checkout Avani late morning. 10–15 min transfer. Check in Deluxe Suite. Spa afternoon.
    Arrange spa the night before or at Anana check-in. Transfer car already set when hotels were booked.
  5. Day 5 — Anana · buffer — Pool / suite. Optional Nopparat Thara or Tiger Cave (hotel car, early, modest dress). Quiet night.
  6. Day 6 — to Koh Lanta — Morning van + ferry to Saladan (2.5–3.5 hrs). Beach only after check-in.
    Arrange: hotel-to-hotel transfer when Lanta is booked. Leave Anana with time to spare.
  7. Day 7 — Lanta — Long Beach sunset dinner in walking distance if you stayed north.
  8. Day 8 — Lanta Old Town — Hotel car, half day. Book the night before.
  9. Day 9 — Koh Rok — Only if seas are calm.
    Arrange 48 hours out at the Lanta desk. Cancel if the forecast is ugly.
  10. Day 10 — Rest / spa.
  11. Day 11 — Extra beach day.
  12. Day 12 — Saladan → KBV → home. Leave 4+ hours before a domestic hop, 6+ before BKK international.

Flights reminder

In / out KBV. Skip any BKK layover under ~2.5 hours (intl → domestic + immigration). TG 346 LHE→BKK is often late; do not pair it with a 7:35 AM Krabi hop.
