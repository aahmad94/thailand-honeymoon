Stays

10–12 Jan — Royal Orchid Sheraton, river view king

2 nights. Friends rate ~$139. Request early check-in for the 06:10 arrival. Leave the hotel ~07:30–08:00 on the 12th for the 10:45 KBV hop. Hotel car to BKK.

12–15 Jan — Avani Ao Nang Cliff (3 nights)

Boat days. Mid cliff / sea-view, not the $2,500 suite. Walk to the longtail pier.

15–18 Jan — Anana Deluxe Suite (3 nights)

Spa + quieter room. 10–15 min from Avani. Extra night vs the old plan because the first Ao Nang block is now 6 nights.

18–22 Jan — Koh Lanta north (4 nights / 5 days)

Arrive 18th. Leave the morning of the 22nd. Layana first pick, Avani+ backup. Net beach time is enough for Old Town + one boat or a lot of sand — not both at full speed.

22–23 Jan — Ao Nang one night

Holiday Inn or a standard Avani room. Do not rebook Anana — you want 30–40 min to KBV at 09:00 on the 23rd.
